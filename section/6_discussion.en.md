# Discussion
Here we describe the issues drawn from the evaluation results.
## The Need for Evaluation That Includes Existing Threats, Not Just AI Threats
With MAESTRO, AI threats could be evaluated in detail, but the system on which the AI operates was not evaluated much. From the standpoint of protecting the system, it is necessary to evaluate not only AI threats but also the system on which the AI operates, and carrying out the evaluation of AI threats and system threats at the same time, rather than separately, makes it possible to grasp the threats to the system as a whole.

## Documents Required for Threat Modeling
In this study, we prepared an overview, a context diagram, a DFD, and data details, and evaluated each technique. For STRIDE and STRIDE+AI, these documents alone were sufficient, but when carrying out MAESTRO, having documents that go deeper into the internal workings of the AI would help.

## Knowledge Required of Participants Carrying Out Threat Modeling
In threat modeling, it is desirable for participants from various standpoints, such as the red team and the blue team, to exchange opinions, but when the focus is on AI, at least one participant with deep knowledge of AI security is required. Alternatively, it is desirable to provide classroom training or the like on AI threats before carrying out the threat modeling.
